## Brand & Style
This design system establishes a high-precision, cybernetic agritech aesthetic engineered for mission-critical AIoT autonomous greenhouse and biosensor operations. The UI fuses industrial telemetry command centers with organic biosensing intelligence, balancing scientific discipline with luminous cyber-agritech signals.

### Personality & Values
- **Precision Agritech:** Clinical accuracy, sensory real-time responsiveness, and analytical clarity.
- **Symbiotic High-Tech:** Merging silicon computational intelligence with chlorophyll vitality.
- **Sovereign Alertness:** Absolute situational clarity via stark contrast, instant fault isolation, and luminescent status indicators.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic synthesizes **Glassmorphic Cyber-Minimalism** with **Telemetry HUD Interface Architecture**:
- Sub-surface obsidian depths overlaid with frosted, low-luminance glass panels.
- Micro-fine hairline containment grids evoking laboratory measurement reticles and microfluidic channels.
- Luminescent signals: glowing emerald micro-pills, pulse vectors, and stark crimson alert diagnostics that pierce through ambient shadows without causing sensory fatigue.

## Colors
The color hierarchy is engineered for prolonged operational monitoring in low-light environments, prioritizing immediate spectral recognition of biosensor anomalies and operational vitality.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#10B981` / Emerald Prime):** Denotes physiological homeostasis, active micro-irrigation lines, nominal soil nitrogen-phosphorus-potassium (NPK) ratios, and streaming sensor health.
- **Secondary (`#EF4444` / Crimson Critical):** Reserved strictly for critical biological distress, leaf blight detection, desiccation threats, actuator valve failures, and telemetry dropouts.
- **Tertiary (`#06B6D4` / Cyber Cyan):** Utilized for ambient atmospheric telemetry (vapor-pressure deficit, PAR photon flux density, and algorithmic predictive vectors).
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0A0D0F` / Deep Obsidian):** The foundational substrate. Elevated structural tiers employ `#11161B` (Surface Medium) and `#161F26` (Surface High) to construct depth without chromatic pollution.
- **Borders & Reticles (`#1E293B` & `#1F2937`):** Hairline structure lines calibrated at low opacity to partition high-density data matrices cleanly.

## Typography
Typography bridges computational mechanics with ergonomic analytical legibility. A tripartite typographic structure is strictly enforced:

1. **Space Grotesk (Structural Titles & Section Headers):** Delivers a technical, slightly retro-futuristic geometric character that accents modular telemetry quadrants and dashboard sector indicators.
2. **Inter (Interface & Contextual Narratives):** Provides maximum neutral legibility for analytical insights, agronomic diagnostic readouts, and systemic notifications.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Sensor Streams, Statuses & HUD Data):** Applied to all dynamic metric streams, sensor units, coordinates, timestamps, and pill metadata. Monospaced tabular alignment guarantees rock-solid visual stability during rapid real-time telemetry refreshes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout implements a high-density, modular **Fluid HUD Grid** calibrated for multi-pane analytical oversight.

### Grid & Density Hierarchy
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic framework with `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem) and `margin-desktop` (2.5rem). Optimized for simultaneous multi-quadrant visualization: environmental stream arrays, dynamic spider/radar charts, and crop canopy maps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout. Aggregates peripheral charts into modular collapsible tabs while maintaining continuous visibility over critical alert cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column single-stream telemetry stack. Vital signs, plant distress toggles, and critical actions pin to top and bottom edge corridors.

### Spacing Rhythm
Built upon a strict 4px base increment (`0.25rem`). Component internal pads rely on `space-xs` and `space-sm` for dense HUD metric chips, expanding to `space-md` and `space-lg` inside analytical cards to preserve optical separation between unrelated telemetry groups.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy skeuomorphic cast shadows, opting instead for **Sub-Surface Glassmorphic Stratification** with tinted edge luminance.

### Depth Layers
- **Substrate (Level 0 - `#0A0D0F`):** Deep canvas space featuring faint vector coordinate gridlines and micro-dot meshes rendered at 4% opacity.
- **Standard Telemetry Tile (Level 1 - `#11161B` at 80% opacity):** Backdropped with a `12px` frosted blur and framed with a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #1E293B`).
- **Floating Diagnostic Overlay (Level 2 - `#161F26` at 90% opacity):** Backdropped with a `20px` blur, bordered with `1px solid #334155`, and anchored by an ambient diffused shadow: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Active / Alert Modulation (Glow Elevation):**
  - **Healthy/Nominal state:** Perimeter highlights cast an emerald atmospheric bloom: `box-shadow: 0 0 16px -2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
  - **Critical Alert state:** Perimeter borders shift to crimson with an urgent, oscillating outer pulse: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px -2px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.4)`.

## Shapes
The design adopts a **Soft Industrial Geometry (Level 1)**. Elements maintain precise, disciplined contours that communicate structural robustness without the aggression of pure 0px brutalist angles.

### Radii Rules
- **Base Cards & Graph Panels:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`), delivering crisp, technical separation.
- **Telemetry Indicators, Inputs & Micro-Panels:** `0.25rem` (`rounded`), echoing physical laboratory display modules.
- **Status Pills & Live Stream Markers:** Fully radiused (`9999px` capsule form) to contrast organic telemetry states against the rectangular analytical architecture.

## Components

### Telemetry Cards & Metric HUDs
- **Base Construction:** Glassmorphic substrate (`#11161B` at 80% alpha) with hairline border (`#1E293B`). Top right corners contain monospaced coordinate/node signatures (`[NODE_04.88]`).
- **Internal Typography:** Category labels use `label-sm` in muted slate (`#64748B`), with dynamic values set in `telemetry-display` in crisp off-white (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Interactive State:** Hover invokes subtle border transition from `#1E293B` to `#334155` alongside a faint emerald or cyan gradient top sheen.

### Buttons & Actuator Triggers
- **Primary (Emerald Actuator):** Semi-translucent `#10B981` base with `1px solid #34D399`, font `JetBrains Mono` bold uppercase. On hover, expands an inner radiant neon glow with zero lateral shift.
- **Critical / Override (Crimson Alert):** Deep `#1F1315` base bordered by `#EF4444`. Typography set in crimson. Engaged state triggers high-contrast `#EF4444` solid fill with bright white text.
- **Ghost HUD Controls:** Transparent background, `1px solid #1E293B` border, text `#94A3B8`. Hover introduces `#161F26` surface fill and text in `#F8FAFC`.

### Telemetry Pill Badges
- Encapsulated pills (`rounded-full`) composed of `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.
- Employs a dual-element visual indicator: an animated 6px breathing beacon dot on the left, paired with `label-sm` uppercase telemetry metadata (`NPK OPTIMAL`, `IRRIGATION ACTIVE`, `DESICCATION HAZARD`).
- Healthy: Green tint background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), neon green text (`#34D399`), glowing green beacon.
- Hazard: Red tint background (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`), bright red text (`#F87171`), pulsating crimson beacon.

### Form Inputs & Slider Controls
- Input fields display dark inset surfaces (`#0D1217`) with recessed internal shadows and `#1E293B` borders. Monospaced input values glow faintly when focused (`#10B981`).
- Sliders feature micro-calibrated tick marks for moisture, temperature, and nutrient dose controls. The thumb is a geometric diamond or pill with an active emerald neon core.

### Visualizations: Spider Charts & Telemetry Sparklines
- **Spider/Radar Biosensor Charts:** Web axes mapped in `#1E293B` with monospaced axis tags (`VPD`, `PAR`, `TEMP`, `RH`, `CO2`, `PH`). Data polygon filled with `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)` anchored by a `1.5px solid #10B981` boundary and glowing vertex nodules.
- **Sparklines & Area Streams:** Gradient-filled paths dissolving from `#10B981` (or `#EF4444` in failure zones) into pure transparent obsidian canvas, backed by hairline vertical time gridlines.
